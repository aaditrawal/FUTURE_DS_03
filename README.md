# FUTURE_DS_03
Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard 


Project Objective
To analyze ecommerce sales data and understand:
• Total sales, quantity, profit, and AOV
• Category and sub-category performance
• Payment mode distribution
• Customer contribution to sales
• State-wise profit trends
• Monthly profit fluctuations
• Quarter-wise filtering and trend comparison

Tools & Techniques Used
Power BI:
→ KPI Cards (Total Amount, Quantity, Profit, AOV)
→ Donut Charts (Payment Mode & Category Distribution)
→ Bar Charts (Top Sub-Categories & Top Customers)
→ State-wise Profit Analysis
→ Monthly Profit Trend Chart
→ Quarter Filters & Interactive Slicers
→ Conditional formatting and dashboard theming

Key Insights From the Dashboard
✔ Total Sales Amount: 438K+
✔ Total Quantity Sold: 5,615 units
✔ Total Profit: 37K+
✔ Clothing category contributes the highest share of sales
✔ COD is the most used payment mode
✔ Printers and Bookcases are top sub-categories
✔ Uttar Pradesh leads in overall profit contribution
✔ Profit shows strong spikes in January and November
✔ A few key customers contribute a large portion of revenue
